# Third-party materials

## TodoMVC

Source: https://github.com/tastejs/todomvc

Pinned tag 1.3.1, commit d69828232d2ab3881d33d41d3a4df4ed7947c7d7. The adapted Vanilla ES6 examples include the upstream compiled bundle and styles. Each fixture retains its PROVENANCE.json and TODOMVC_LICENSE.md MIT notice. Asset paths and the authored focus-view lab panel differ from the upstream page; the compiled bundle remains unchanged.

The injected focus-view defect is authored for this demonstration. It is not an upstream bug discovery. Do not remove the notices when redistributing the included examples.

## Fonts

IBM Plex Sans and JetBrains Mono are served locally from prototype_ui/fonts/. They were obtained through Google Fonts. Their SIL Open Font License notices are retained alongside the font files as ibmplexsans-OFL.txt and jetbrainsmono-OFL.txt.

## Design assistance

Design guidance was consulted from anthropics/skills, vercel-labs/agent-skills and nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill. Those installed skill packages are not bundled into this repository. The mint, charcoal, coral and cyan palette was adapted from a user-supplied visual reference; the reference image is not included in the repository.

## Dependencies

Python packages are installed from requirements.txt. Playwright installs Chromium separately. Docker FalkorDB and Ollama are external services; their respective licenses apply.
